## Kirchhoff and Bunsen
- Kirchhoff first published the reversal of the sodium and lithium lines in a communication to the Berlin Academy on 27 Oct 1859 | 1859 | Kirchhoff, Phil. Mag. (4) 20, 1-21 (1860), note on p. 19, doi 10.1080/14786446008642901 (archive.org bub_gb_UFRGAAAAcAAJ) | verified
- Drummond (lime) light through a salt flame: bright sodium lines become dark at the place of Fraunhofer's D; sunlight through a sodium flame makes the D lines darker and broader; hence sodium in the Sun's atmosphere; three arguments against a terrestrial origin (not enough gaseous sodium in air; no change near horizon; D absent in some stars) | 1860 | same, pp. 15-17 | verified
