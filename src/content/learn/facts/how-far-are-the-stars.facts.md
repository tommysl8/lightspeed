## The ancient argument
- Aristotle, On the Heavens II.14: "there would have to be passings and turnings of the fixed stars. Yet no such thing is observed." | 4th c. BCE | J. L. Stocks translation, MIT Classics, https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/heavens.2.ii.html | verified (primary, translation)
