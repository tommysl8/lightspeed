# Code of conduct

Skyfold is a place to share curiosity about the universe. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or level of
experience.

**Please do:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people whose first language isn't English.
- Assume good faith, and critique ideas and code, not people.
- Back up physics and data claims with sources, and accept corrections gracefully.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

**If something goes wrong:** email the maintainer at tommysliu8@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking
them.

This code is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
